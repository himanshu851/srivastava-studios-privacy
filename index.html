Privacy Policy

Arrow Trails: Logic Escape

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Srivastava Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops Arrow Trails: Logic Escape (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Game stores information on your device and how third-party services, including Google advertising, consent, and purchase services, may handle information when their features are used. Practices may vary between testing and released versions.

1. Information Handled by the Game

Local gameplay data

The Game saves information primarily on your device, including:

  • Completed and unlocked levels, puzzle progress, and game statistics.
  • Settings, preferences, hint usage, and gameplay state.
  • Locally recorded purchase-entitlement status, where applicable.

We do not currently operate a player-login system, cloud-save server, or developer-operated service that automatically uploads gameplay progress. An optional statistics export, where provided, is initiated by the player. Data processed only on your device is different from information transmitted by third-party SDKs.

Technical information handled by third parties

Depending on the installed version, enabled features, consent choices, and applicable Google SDK behavior, third-party services may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • App launch, advertising, and other app interactions.
  • Technical diagnostics, performance information, and crash-related information where applicable.
  • Device, app, or service-related identifiers where available.
  • Purchase status, product identifiers, and transaction-related information when Google Play Billing is used.

The Game does not request precise GPS-location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. It does not require you to provide a name, email address, or phone number to play.

Information you choose to send us

If you contact us for help or privacy questions, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use it to respond and address the request.

2. Advertising and Consent

Some versions of the Game include Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements for hints or continuing a puzzle.

Internal monetization testing: A testing version uses Google's sample ad units rather than live production ad units. These are still requests to Google's advertising SDK and should not be interpreted as meaning no technical information is processed. Advertising requests are gated by the consent status and permissions provided through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where required.

When required or available in your region, the Game provides applicable advertising-consent and privacy options. Your choices can affect which advertising requests are permitted. Advertising and privacy controls may differ by region and SDK configuration.

A permanent ad-removal purchase, when successfully owned, suppresses advertising requests made by the Game. The current internal-testing design provides limited ad-free hint and Continue functionality for eligible ad-free owners.

Production advertising is disabled in the internal monetization-testing candidate. We will update our disclosures if production advertising practices change.

3. In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing

The Game may offer optional one-time purchases through Google Play Billing, such as extra chances per level attempt, permanent ad removal, or a combined upgrade. Purchases are optional; the core puzzle game does not require an account.

Google processes checkout and payment information under its own policies. The Game does not request or store your credit-card number. To provide or restore purchased features, the Game and Google Play Billing may process product IDs, purchase status, and purchase tokens. The Game uses purchase information for acknowledgment and restoration, and locally stores entitlement records; it does not log raw purchase tokens.

Important for testers: Google Play license testers using designated test payment instruments can test purchases without normal charges. Simply joining an internal-testing track does not make purchases free. Other accounts or ordinary payment methods may incur real charges. Check the Google Play payment dialog before confirming any transaction.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game integrates services provided by Google, as applicable to the installed version:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertisements, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable consent and privacy controls.
  • Google Play Billing for one-time purchases, acknowledgment, and restoration.
  • Google Play and related platform services for installation, distribution, and device functionality.

These services may process technical, interaction, identifier, or transaction-related information under their applicable terms and policies. The specific information processed depends on the SDK version and enabled features.

For information about Google's practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Why Information Is Handled

Information may be used to:

  • Save puzzle progress and maintain game preferences locally.
  • Provide puzzle hints, rewards, and purchased entitlements.
  • Display and measure advertisements when permitted.
  • Process, acknowledge, and restore Google Play purchases.
  • Support app reliability, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not operate our own advertising-data resale business.

6. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Game progress and preferences are primarily stored in local application storage. They ordinarily remain until they are reset, cleared, or removed through device/app controls, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Clearing app storage may permanently erase saved progress.

If you would like to request deletion of personal information held by Srivastava Studios, contact us at popularbazaar9@gmail.com with the subject Arrow Trails Data Deletion Request. Describe the information you want removed and provide enough details for us to locate any records under our control. Do not send payment-card details or passwords.

We can review and delete information we control, subject to legal or operational retention obligations. We cannot directly erase data independently held by Google or other third-party services. Google's own retention, deletion, and account controls apply to those records. Purchase or transaction records may need to be retained by the relevant payment provider for legal, tax, refund, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Support messages are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to requests and meet applicable obligations. We do not claim that all SDK data is automatically deleted within 90 days.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary access to information and keep gameplay data on the device where practical. Google advertising and purchase services provide transport and security measures for their SDK communications, including encryption in transit where documented. We do not independently guarantee every third-party data flow or storage system.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely risk-free.

8. Age and Children's Privacy

Arrow Trails: Logic Escape is designed and marketed for audiences aged 13 and older. We do not intentionally request personal contact information from children through gameplay. Applicable regional consent and advertising requirements may differ, including for some users aged 13–17.

Parents or guardians may contact us with questions about the Game or information under our control.

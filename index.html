1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Trails: Logic Escape, developed by Srivastava Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game, including information stored locally and data that may be processed by integrated third-party services.

2. Information Handled by the Game

Local Gameplay Data

The game stores certain information on your device to provide gameplay and maintain progress, including:

  • Completed and unlocked levels
  • Game progress and puzzle statistics
  • Gameplay settings and preferences
  • Hint usage and related gameplay state
  • Locally recorded purchase entitlement state, where applicable

We have not implemented a developer-operated account system, cloud save server, or automatic gameplay-data upload service in the current game. Statistics export, where available, is initiated by the player.

Technical and Device Information

The application includes third-party Google SDK components for advertising, consent management, and in-app billing.

Depending on SDK initialization, configuration, and enabled features, Google services may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP
  • Application interactions and usage information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device or app-related identifiers, where available

The game does not request precise GPS location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. Removing an advertising-ID permission does not necessarily prevent all technical data processing by third-party SDKs.

Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry.

3. Advertising and In-App Purchases

Current Play Testing Version: Advertisement requests and in-app purchase transactions are disabled in this testing build.

The application contains Google advertising and billing integrations intended for possible use in later releases.

If advertising is enabled in a future release, Google advertising services may process information for ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, and other applicable purposes. Where required, consent choices and privacy controls will be provided.

If in-app purchases are enabled in a future release, transactions will be processed through Google Play Billing. We do not directly collect or store payment card numbers in the game.

We will review and update this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures before activating those features.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is handled for purposes including:

  • Saving gameplay progress and preferences
  • Operating puzzle and reward features
  • Responding to support and privacy requests
  • Maintaining reliability and security
  • Supporting advertising or billing functionality only when those features are enabled

We do not operate an independent advertising-data resale service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data is primarily stored in the game's local application storage. It generally remains there until it is reset, cleared, or removed by uninstalling the application, subject to applicable device and operating-system backup behavior.

You may manage local app data using the controls available on your device. Clearing application storage may permanently remove saved progress.

We retain support correspondence only as reasonably necessary to address inquiries and meet applicable legal obligations.

To request deletion of information that we control, contact us using the email address below. Data independently processed by Google may also be subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary access to information and avoid collecting information not needed for the game's features. Integrated Google services may use encrypted network connections when transmitting data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The game does not require users to create accounts or submit personal contact details to play.

We do not intentionally ask children to provide personal information through gameplay. Where applicable, age-related privacy protections and advertising requirements will be followed before advertising features are enabled.

Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or deletion requests.
